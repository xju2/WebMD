# Welcome

This is the **WebMD sandbox**, a small workspace for trying things out. Edit,
rename, and delete anything here. Your changes stay in this copy and never
touch your real notes.

WebMD opens the sandbox when `WORKSPACE_ROOT` is not set. To open your own
notes, add a line like this to `~/.webmd.conf` and restart WebMD:

```conf
WORKSPACE_ROOT=/absolute/path/to/notes
```

> [!info] Starting over
> The sandbox is copied out of WebMD the first time it runs. Delete that copy
> and restart WebMD to get a fresh one. The server prints where it is on
> startup.
