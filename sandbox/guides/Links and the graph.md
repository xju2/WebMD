# Links and the graph

Type `[[` to link another note. WebMD suggests notes as you type and writes the
shortest link that still finds the one you picked: [[Garden planner]].

- Link to a heading: [[Writing notes#Math]]
- Give a link its own text: [[Tasks|the task guide]]
- A link to a note that does not exist yet is drawn wavy:
  [[Seed catalogue]]. Click it to create the note.

## Embeds

Put `!` in front of a link to show that note, or one of its sections, inline:

![[Garden planner#Beds]]

## Linked mentions

The bottom of every note lists the notes that link to it. Open
[[Garden planner]] and scroll down to see this note there.

## The graph

The graph view in the rail draws every note and the links between them.
Notes that cite a paper are connected to it too.
