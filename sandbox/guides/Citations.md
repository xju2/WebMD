# Citations

Keep BibTeX entries in `references.bib` at the workspace root and cite them
with Pandoc syntax. Typing `@` suggests the keys it knows.

Transformers replaced recurrence with attention [@Vaswani:2017], and graph
networks carry the same idea over to relational data [@Battaglia:2018].

Hover a citation in the preview for its details. The preview adds a References
section at the end of the note.

Pasting an arXiv, DOI, or INSPIRE link imports its BibTeX entry and replaces
the link with a citation. That needs a network connection.
